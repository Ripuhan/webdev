My Favorite Places

Welcome to my travel bucket list! Here are three amazing destinations I would love to visit and explore someday.

Quick Jump

  1. Tokyo, Japan
  2. Iceland
  3. Paris, France

Tokyo, Japan

Things to do:

  • Explore the electronic and anime paradise of Akihabara.
  • Eat authentic, fresh sushi at the Tsukiji Outer Market.
  • Visit the historic Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa.

To learn more about this incredible, fast-paced city, visit the Tokyo Wikipedia page.

Iceland

Things to do:

  • Relax in the mineral-rich, geothermal waters of the Blue Lagoon.
  • Hunt for the mesmerizing Northern Lights (Aurora Borealis) during the winter.
  • Drive the Golden Circle to see spectacular geysers and national parks.

If you want to read up on its unique geography and volcanic activity, check out the Iceland Wikipedia page.

Paris, France

Things to do:

  • Take a trip to the top of the iconic Eiffel Tower for a city-wide view.
  • Admire classical art and the Mona Lisa at the Louvre Museum.
  • Enjoy fresh croissants and espresso at a local Parisian sidewalk café.

For more deep-rooted history, fashion, and culture, head over to the Paris Wikipedia page.
